Refund & Cancellation Policy

Please read our policies carefully before placing your order with Cake Factory Kota.

1. Refund Policy

At Cake Factory, customer satisfaction is our top priority.
• If there is any defect, quality issue, or difference in the cake delivered (including taste, design, or freshness), the customer is eligible for a full refund of the amount paid
• Customer must report the issue within 24 hours of delivery with appropriate proof (such as photos, videos)
•Once the issue is verified by our team, the entire amount will be refunded to the original payment method within a reasonable time frame.

2. Quality and Satisfaction Guarantee

At Cake Factory Kota, we take great pride in our baking. Every cake is crafted with premium ingredients and strict quality control. If you receive a product that is defective, spoiled, or significantly different from what was described or agreed upon (e.g., incorrect flavor or major design error), please contact us within 2 hours of receiving the order.

3. Damage During Transit

If Cake Factory Kota handles the delivery, we guarantee the cake reaches your location safely. If the cake is damaged during our delivery process, we will replace it (if time permits) or issue a full refund. However, if you or your designated representative choose to pick up the cake from our bakery, Cake Factory Kota is no longer responsible for any damage that occurs once the product leaves our premises.

4. Processing Refunds

Approved refunds will be processed back to the original method of payment (such as Razorpay, UPI, or original Card). Please allow 5-7 business days for the amount to reflect in your account, depending on your bank's processing times.

5. Non-Refundable Items

Certain aesthetic items like customized fondant toppers, specialized cake boards, and edible printing that have already been produced specifically for your order cannot be refunded once processing has started.

If you need to request a cancellation or refund, please reach out to our team immediately at cakefactorykota@gmail.com or call us at our store numbers.
